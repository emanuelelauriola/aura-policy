Privacy Policy for AURA

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

AURA ("we", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

Microphone Access & Audio Data

AURA requires access to your device’s microphone strictly for real-time digital signal processing (DSP). Audio streams are processed locally in real-time on your device and are never recorded, stored, transmitted, or shared on any server.

Authentication & Data Collection

We use Firebase Authentication (provided by Google) to manage user sign-in options (Guest access, Google, Apple, and Email). We only collect minimum necessary account metadata (such as user ID) required for secure authentication. We do not track, profile, or sell user data.
